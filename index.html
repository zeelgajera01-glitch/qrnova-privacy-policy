1. Overview

QRNova is a QR code and barcode scanning and generation app. Its features may let you scan codes, create codes for text, links, Wi-Fi, contacts, phone numbers, email, messages, UPI details, locations or events, and keep a history or favorites on your device.

QRNova does not require you to create an account to use the core scanning and QR-generation features in the version covered by this policy. We do not sell your personal information.

This policy applies to the QRNova Android application. It does not cover websites or third-party apps that you open or use through a QR code.

2. Camera and scanning

QRNova requests camera permission when you use its scanner. The camera is used to detect and read QR codes and barcodes. The app includes Google ML Kit barcode-scanning components for this function.

Camera access is not needed for QR-generation features. You can deny or revoke camera permission in your Android settings; scanning with the live camera will then be unavailable.

QRNova is designed to analyze camera frames for code detection on your device. The app does not need to retain camera video or photos to provide live scanning. If you choose an image from another app or use an Android sharing feature, that other app's handling of the image is governed by its own privacy policy.

3. QR content and information you enter

A QR code can contain information such as a name, phone number, email address, website, Wi-Fi network details, UPI/payment text, event details, location coordinates or other text. Treat the contents of a QR code as information that may be sensitive.

  • Scanning: QRNova reads the content encoded in the code so it can show the scan result and offer relevant actions.
  • Creating: Information you type into a generator is used to create the QR code you requested.
  • History and favorites: If you save a result or mark it as a favorite, the app may keep that item in local app storage on your device so it is available in the app.
  • Sharing: When you choose to share, copy, open or send a result, the information is passed to the app or destination you select. That destination then handles it under its own terms and privacy policy.

Do not create or share QR codes containing information that you are not authorized to use or disclose. Before sharing a Wi-Fi, payment, contact or location code, check its contents and intended recipient.

4. App diagnostics, analytics and Firebase services

The QRNova package provided for review includes Google Firebase components for Analytics, Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring, Cloud Messaging, Remote Config and In-App Messaging. The information processed depends on which services are enabled and used in the published version.

When enabled, these services may process technical and usage information such as:

  • App version, device model, Android version, language and general device or app configuration;
  • App launches, sessions, feature interactions and other app-usage events;
  • Crash logs, diagnostic details and information needed to investigate app errors;
  • Performance measurements, app startup information and network request timing;
  • Firebase app-instance or installation identifiers; and
  • A Firebase Cloud Messaging token and delivery-related information if push messaging is enabled.

These services are provided by Google LLC and its affiliates. Google may process information as described in its Firebase privacy and security information and Google Privacy Policy.

QRNova's inspected Android package requests internet access. Network access is used by online service components when those components are enabled. QRNova does not request Android's fine or coarse location permission in the package reviewed. If you enter location coordinates into a location QR generator, those coordinates form part of the QR content you choose to create.

5. Advertising and consent

The Android package provided for review contains Google's User Messaging Platform and advertising-identifier-related Android permissions. The inspected package did not contain an identifiable Google Mobile Ads serving SDK. If advertising is added or enabled in a future version, this policy will be updated to identify the advertising services and explain the information they may process. Where a consent message is presented, you can manage the choices offered there.

Before publishing this policy, the developer should confirm that the production release does not include another advertising SDK or ad-serving integration that is not reflected in the inspected package.

6. Sharing and third-party services

We may disclose technical information to service providers that operate app functions described in this policy, such as Google Firebase. These providers process information under their own service terms and privacy documentation.

QRNova does not control third-party websites, payment apps, messaging apps, browsers, Wi-Fi settings or other apps you choose to open from a scan result. Review the privacy terms of those services before sending information to them.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to limit the information handled by QRNova to what is needed for its features. Information saved by the app is generally kept within the app's storage area on your device. Online service providers may use their own security measures, including encryption in transit where supported.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Keep your device protected and avoid scanning or sharing codes from sources you do not trust.

8. Data retention and deletion

  • Local history and favorites: You can remove saved items using the app's available controls. Clearing QRNova's app storage or uninstalling the app may also remove locally saved information.
  • Device permissions: You can revoke camera or notification permission at any time in Android settings. Revoking a permission may disable the related feature.
  • Diagnostics and service data: Information processed by Firebase is retained according to the relevant Google/Firebase service settings and retention rules. You may contact us with a privacy request using the contact details below.

9. Children's privacy

QRNova is a general-purpose utility and is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly ask children to submit personal information to us. If you believe a child has provided personal information through QRNova, contact us so we can review the request.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app features, service providers or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date. Please review this page periodically.
